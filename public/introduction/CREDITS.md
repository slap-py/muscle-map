# Introduction model stills

Captured from Fabrica's own aligned left upper/lower leg viewer by
`scripts/capture-introduction-models.mjs`. Anatomy and Skeleton show the same
camera view, in Light and Dim themes. These are static screenshots, with labels,
not additional model data. No anatomy has been generated for this page.

The pictured models are adapted from Z-Anatomy (Gauthier Kervyn, CC BY-SA 4.0)
and BodyParts3D (The Database Center for Life Science; original model Kousaku
Okubo, CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan). Adapted model assets and these stills: CC BY-SA 4.0.

Source objects, regional crops, reflection of the left lower leg, mesh processing,
and illustrative attachment footprints are documented on the app's `#/credits`
page and in `public/models/CREDITS.md` and the region manifests.
